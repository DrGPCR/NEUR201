## The story of this unit

A dentist injects **lidocaine**, and within minutes your tooth goes numb. How? This unit follows that
question from a single neuron to a whole population of them:

- **W5 L2** — you record from a model pain neuron with and without lidocaine, and work out *which ion
  channel* the drug acts on.
- **W6 L2** — you ask whether lidocaine-treated neurons are genuinely *unusual*, or whether untreated
  neurons could fire that slowly just by natural variation.
- **Unit 2 Data Analysis Assignment** — you test an unknown compound, **Drug Y**, on model pain neurons,
  and work out what it does and which ion channel it acts on.
- **Unit 2 Statistics Assignment** — you ask whether Drug Y's effect is genuinely *unusual*, by comparing
  200 treated neurons with 200 untreated ones.
